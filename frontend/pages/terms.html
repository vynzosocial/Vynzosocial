1. Terms & Conditions

  • Age: You must be 18 years old or older to create a Vynzo account. Accounts found to belong to anyone under 18 will be removed.
  • Your account: Keep your password secure. You are responsible for activity on your account. Your Vynzo ID is private and must not be shared.
  • Your content: You own what you post, and you give Vynzo permission to store and display it so the service can work. Only post content you have the right to share.
  • Enforcement: Vynzo may remove content, restrict features or suspend accounts that break these rules.
  • Changes: These terms may be updated. Continuing to use Vynzo after an update means you accept the new version.

2. Community Guidelines

  • Be respectful. No harassment, threats, hate speech or bullying.
  • No sexual content involving minors, no sexual exploitation, and no sharing of private images without consent.
  • No violence, graphic abuse, or content that encourages self-harm or illegal activity.
  • No spam, scams, fake accounts or impersonation.
  • Respect copyright and other people's privacy. Do not post someone's personal information.
  • Use blogs, stories, posts and live streams honestly. Report anything that breaks these rules.

3. Regulations

  • You must follow the laws that apply where you live when using Vynzo.
  • Content that is illegal, or that Vynzo is required to remove, will be removed and may be reported to the authorities.
  • You can appeal a moderation decision where an appeal option is offered.
